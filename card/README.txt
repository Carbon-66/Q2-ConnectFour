CONNECT FOUR for the Shanling Q2 (Q2 Pod)
=========================================

Drop discs into the board in turns. The first to line up four of their own colour, across, up
and down or on a slant, wins. If the board fills first, it's a draw.

INSTALL
1. Copy the .connect4 folder to the root of the card. On a Mac, press Cmd-Shift-. in Finder to
   see dot-folders.
2. Eject the card and open Library -> Games -> Connect Four.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
  Play vs. AI          You're red, the AI is yellow. You take turns going first.
  Play with a Friend  Pass the Q2 back and forth: red and yellow, taking turns going first.

AI LEVEL (on the main menu; press Centre on it to change it)
  Easy     Looks one move ahead and doesn't always spot your four coming. Good for kids.
  Medium   Always takes a win and blocks yours, but often misses a better move.
  Hard     Looks six moves ahead and never slips. It can still be beaten.

CONTROLS
  Wheel          move the disc over the columns (and the highlight in menus)
  Centre         drop the disc (pick, in menus)
  Touch          put a finger on a column to aim, lift to drop; tap menu items
  Return         pause: Resume, Take Back (your last move), Restart, Main Menu
  Quit           on the main menu: leave the game
  Hold Return    leave the game (Q2 Pod's own)
The music keeps playing: Play/Pause and the side buttons stay the player's.

FILES
  q2-connect4    the game
  connect4.sav   your AI level and the last way you played
  connect4.log   what the game did, for when something goes wrong
